The Studio

Spaces that hold the light, and the people inside them.

Aura Design is a team of architects, interior designers and landscape specialists working under one roof. We believe the best rooms are never decorated after the fact. They are composed from the first line of the plan, with structure, light, material and furniture considered together.

Every commission begins with listening. We study how you live or work, then design around those rituals with a restrained palette, honest materials and meticulous detailing that ages gracefully.
